## Violations found and fixed

1. **Deal-count arithmetic error (provenance).** The draft claimed the
   window holds "five unique transactions, not nine" (also "across five
   distinct transactions" in the TL;DR and Key takeaways). The pack lists
   nine raw entries with two duplicate pairs — BrainChild's $116M round
   (`other` + `financing`) and the Novartis-Avidity acquisition (one
   value-tagged, one value-untagged) — so 9 − 2 = **seven** unique
   transactions (five with disclosed totals, two undisclosed). Fixed the
   Hook, TL;DR, comp paragraph, and Key takeaway #1 to say seven unique /
   five value-disclosed.

2. **Unsupported "M&A write-down" (inference).** The Hook called the
   Avidity event "one M&A write-down." No pack field or evidence card
   records a write-down; evidence q1 calls it a "value-destruction event"
   and the draft's own One Take lists a future Avidity impairment as a
   catalyst (i.e. not yet quantified). Changed to "one M&A
   value-destruction event."

3. **Description imprecision.** Frontmatter description said "Novartis's
   $12B Avidity buyout fails Phase 3" — the buyout did not fail a trial,
   its lead asset did. Reworded to "…Novartis's $12B Avidity buyout's lead
   asset fails Phase 3."

4. **Milestone-trigger precision.** Market Reaction said the first $50M
   trigger is "tied to a Phase 2 start"; evidence q3 ties it to the
   "first-patient visit of the Phase 2 study." Corrected to "the
   first-patient visit in the Phase 2 study."
