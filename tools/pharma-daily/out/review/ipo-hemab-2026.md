# Critique — ipo-hemab-2026.md

Scope: no data pack exists for this article, so the linked sources were treated as
the evidence base. Every number/date/name was checked by fetching the primary
links (pricing/closing releases, S-1 / S-1/A / 424B4, Q2 10-Q and ex-99.1, ISTH
release, BTD release, Renaissance Capital, BioPharma Dive, Fierce Biotech) plus a
COAG daily price series from Yahoo Finance (the draft's own stated price source).
Report violations only, per instruction.

## Violations

### Factual errors

1. **HMB-003 "was unveiled in June"** (line 23) — wrong month. The July 14 ISTH
   release states the HMB-003 program was announced "Sunday, July 12"; the Q2
   ex-99.1 states "In July, the Company unveiled HMB-003"; Fierce Biotech (Aug 13)
   states "Last month [July], Hemab unveiled HMB-003." Should be July, not June.

2. **HMB-003 "started its first-in-human Phase 1 ... in August"** (line 23) —
   unsupported / contradicted. As of the Q2 ex-99.1 (Aug 11), the company "plans to
   initiate first-in-human studies in the second half of 2026"; Fierce (Aug 13)
   says the asset is "set to enter the clinic later this year." No fetched source
   says FIH dosing started, let alone in August.

3. **"AI DEN-MAB 13.8%"** (line 25) — this investor name does not appear in any
   fetched source. The 424B4 prospectus summary and all press list the register as
   Access Biotechnology, Avoro Capital Advisors, Deep Track Capital, HealthCap,
   Novo Holdings, RA Capital Management, SMALLCAP World Fund, and Sofinnova —
   none of which matches "AI DEN-MAB." Likely fabricated or garbled (the real
   register omits "Avoro Capital Advisors" / "Access Biotechnology" / "HealthCap,"
   which are the actual names in the prospectus).

4. **"Pre-IPO the company raised ~$346M (Series B $135.2M in 2023, Series C
   $156.9M in October 2025)"** (line 25) — the "$346M" total is correct (424B4:
   "approximately $346.0 million since inception"), but the parenthetical omits
   Series Seed (~$2.9M) and Series A ($55M, 2021 per Fierce). Series B + Series C
   = $292.1M, not $346M, so the parenthetical breakdown does not reconcile to the
   stated total and is misleading. (Series B 2023 and Series C Oct 2025 dates are
   individually confirmed by Fierce / 424B4.)

### Provenance gaps (claims not traceable to a fetched source)

5. **Principal-stockholder percentages** (lines 25; also "Novo Holdings 15.2%"
   in lines 21 and 74) — RA Capital 17.6%, Novo Holdings 15.2%, Sofinnova 8.7%,
   Deep Track 8.0%, SMALLCAP World Fund 7.9% were not verifiable: the 424B4
   "Principal Stockholders" table is beyond the fetchable portion of the filing.
   The 15.2% figure is repeated three times but unconfirmed against a source.

6. **"31 efficacy-evaluable at the October 14, 2025 cutoff"** (line 19) — "34
   patients treated" is confirmed by the July 14 LTE release ("34 participants"),
   and the 19–87% / 100% / 92% figures are confirmed by the 424B4 (Oct 14, 2025
   cutoff). But "31 efficacy-evaluable" appears in no fetched source.

7. **"~108 patients" for Velora Pioneer / HMB-002 (NCT06754852)** (line 23) — no
   fetched source states ~108 patients (the 424B4 describes completed 20 mg and
   50 mg cohorts and an ongoing 150 mg cohort, with no enrollment total).

8. **"S-1/A (Apr 27): 11,764,706 shares" and the "42% build from its first
   amended filing"** (lines 11, 27, 31-33) — the cited S-1/A link
   (`d50309ds1a.htm`) is Amendment No. 3 dated April 30, showing 15,000,000
   shares, not 11,764,706. The 11,764,706 base and "Apr 27" date are not in the
   linked filings; they are only indirectly consistent with BioPharma Dive's
   "upped the size ... by 27%" (11.76M → 15M).

9. **"June 26 ISTH presentation announcement"** (lines 43, 78) — the June 26 date
   for the announcement is not in any fetched source (the ISTH congress and data
   releases are July 12–14). The price math for "+13.5% to $36.73 on June 29"
   checks out (Jun 26 close $32.37 → Jun 29 close $36.73 = +13.5%).

### Minor notes (not hard errors)

10. **"1.18x the class median"** (line 39) — computed as $346.7M (greenshoe-inclusive)
    ÷ $295M median (base gross per the parent post). Comparing greenshoe-inclusive
    proceeds to a base median slightly inflates the multiple; defensible but worth
    a footnote.
