## Violations

1. **Broken link — Craig Tendler 8-K.** The inline link (body, "What would change the story") and Sources entry point to `…/000119312526365132/ck0001657677-20260622.htm`. That URL returns SEC "NoSuchKey" (404). The correct document in that accession is `ck0001657677-20260822.htm`. As cited, the link is dead for readers.

2. **Date inconsistency — Tendler appointment.** The body says the board added Tendler on **August 22**; the Sources list says **"8-K, August 25, 2026"**. Both refer to the same filing (date of report Aug 22, filed Aug 25), but the article uses the two dates interchangeably without distinguishing event vs filing date.

3. **Date inconsistency — CMO departure 8-K.** The body says **"August 28 8-K"**; the Sources list says **"8-K, September 2, 2026"**. Same filing again: date of report Aug 28, filed Sep 2. (The substantive fact — CMO Fawzi Benzaghou departs effective September 30 — is correct.)

4. **"Nine days" is not supported by the article's own dates.** The hook says the offering was "upsized twice in the nine days between its first price-range amendment and pricing," but Table 1 dates the first (marketed-range) amendment to **June 4** and pricing to **June 9** — a five-day span, not nine. No linked source supports "nine days."

5. **ERG degrader stage overstated.** The body says the pipeline includes "an ERG degrader in IND-enabling studies." The 424B4 describes both the ERG and allosteric ARON programs as **"preclinical stage"** and says use of proceeds will "progress our ERG and allosteric ARON degraders … into IND-enabling toxicology studies" (future tense). "In IND-enabling studies" is not supported; ERG is preclinical/advancing toward IND-enabling. (The AR-ON "late lead optimization" wording is correct.)

6. **Unverifiable aftermarket absolute marks.** First-day open $33.35 / close $31.60, the Sep 4 close $40.42 (−0.81%, volume 40,378), and the post-IPO range $24.51–41.49 appear in no linked source, and Yahoo Finance returned no data for PBLS when checked. The percentage marks that ARE corroborated by the linked BioBucks source are first-day +58.0% and Aug 28 +95.6%, plus the class marks Eikon −39% / Agomab −18% / Generate −4% and the fourth-place ranking behind Veradermics/Vogenx/Hemab. The absolute closes, volume, and range rest solely on a data feed that is not among the links and could not be independently confirmed.

The unqualified "biotech IPO on record" in the hook is consistent with Fierce but slightly broader than BioPharma Dive's framing cited in the FAQ.
